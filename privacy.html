Privacy policy

Updated October 25, 2026.

Who uses it. TC Google Health is used by one person, Tony Capucille, on his own Google account. It collects no one else's data.

What it reads. Sleep, activity and heart data from Google Health, read only. It cannot write, change or delete anything in Google Health.

Where the data goes. It stays on Tony's own computer and in his own records. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used to train AI models.

Google's rules. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access. Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings.
